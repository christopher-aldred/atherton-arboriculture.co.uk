About the owner

William Atherton — Arboricultural Services

Atherton Arboricultural Services is owned and operated by William Atherton who has over 5 years experience within the industry and holds an FdSc in Arboriculture and Urban Forestry through Myerscough College, currently completing his full BSc in Arboriculture and Urban Forestry. He has worked all over the UK, but mostly operates in the North West of England and is based out of Preston. He also sub contracts as a climber to multiple firms across the North West.
