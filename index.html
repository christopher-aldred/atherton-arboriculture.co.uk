Meet the owner

William Atherton

FdSc Arboriculture & Urban Forestry

Atherton Arboricultural Services is owned and operated by William Atherton who has over 7 years experience within the industry and holds an BSc in Arboriculture and Urban Forestry through Myerscough College. He has worked all over the UK, but mostly operates in the North West of England and is based out of Preston. He also sub contracts as a climber to multiple firms across the North West.
